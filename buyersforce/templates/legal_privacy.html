1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how BuyersForce ("BuyersForce," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you access or use the BuyersForce platform (the "Platform"), as a buyer, seller, or administrator. It applies to everyone who uses the Platform, regardless of where you are located. This Policy is part of, and should be read together with, our End User License Agreement.

2. Information We Collect

Account and profile information you provide: your name, work and personal email addresses, phone number, company, job title, location (state/region and time zone), LinkedIn URL (or a note that you don't have one), password (stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash, never in plain text), and profile photo, if you choose to add one.

Content you create on the Platform: messages and file attachments you send to other users or BuyersForce staff, support requests, evaluation projects, vendor scorecards, notes, and any other content you submit.

Information generated by your use of the Platform: records of actions like evaluation decisions, read/unread message status, and timestamps of activity.

Basic technical information collected automatically by our hosting infrastructure for security and operational purposes, such as IP address and browser type, consistent with standard web server logging.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to: create and administer your account, including reviewing and approving account requests; operate core Platform features such as vendor discovery, evaluations, messaging, scheduling, and our AI assistant "Frankie"; send transactional emails, such as account approvals, invitations, password resets, and new-message or support-ticket alerts; maintain the security and integrity of the Platform; respond to support requests; and comply with legal obligations. Where BuyersForce or Frankie use artificial intelligence to help draft a response to a support request or answer a question, a BuyersForce team member reviews that draft before it is sent to you -- we do not use AI to make automated decisions about your account that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you without human review.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information:

  • with other users as intended by the Platform's design -- for example, a buyer's name and company are visible to a seller they message, and vendor-listing information is visible to buyers;
  • with service providers who perform services on our behalf, as described in Section 7;
  • if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of BuyersForce, our users, or the public; and
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of some or all of our assets, in which case personal information would remain subject to this Policy or a successor policy providing comparable protection.

5. Data Storage, Hosting, and Security

The Platform and its database are hosted in the United States on Railway's cloud infrastructure. We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information, including password hashing and restricted staff access to the underlying database. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward in case you wish to reactivate it or as needed for legitimate business or legal purposes. If your account is denied, revoked, or you ask us to delete your information, we will delete or de-identify it within a reasonable time, except where we need to keep it to comply with a legal obligation, resolve a dispute, or enforce our agreements.

7. Service Providers

We use a small number of service providers to operate the Platform, each of whom is authorized to use your information only as necessary to provide their service to us:

  • Railway -- cloud hosting for the Platform and its database.
  • Resend -- delivery of transactional emails (such as account notifications, invitations, and alerts).
  • Anthropic -- the artificial-intelligence technology behind Frankie and AI-assisted support-ticket drafting.

We do not use advertising, analytics, or marketing platforms, and the Platform does not integrate with Google, Microsoft, Slack, or similar third-party account or productivity services.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Platform uses a single functional cookie to keep you signed in during a session. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or other tracking technologies, and the Platform does not carry third-party advertising.

9. International Users

Buyers and sellers may use the Platform from anywhere in the world. Because the Platform and its database are hosted in the United States, your information will be processed and stored in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country. By using the Platform, you consent to this transfer and processing. If you are located outside the United States and have questions about how this affects you, please contact us using the information in Section 15.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to obtain a copy of it. You can update most of your profile information yourself from your Account settings. To make any other request, contact us at privacy@buyersforce.io. We will take reasonable steps to verify your identity before responding, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (together, "CCPA"), gives you the right to: know what personal information we have collected about you and how we use and share it; delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions; correct inaccurate personal information; and not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is no need to opt out of either. We do not use sensitive personal information for any purpose beyond what is reasonably necessary to provide the Platform, so there is no additional right to limit that applies here. You can exercise any of these rights by contacting us at privacy@buyersforce.io; an authorized agent may do so on your behalf with your written permission. In the preceding twelve months, we have not disclosed personal information to any third party for that third party's own direct marketing purposes, so California's "Shine the Light" law (Civil Code § 1798.83) disclosure does not currently apply, but we will honor any request made under it.

12. Other U.S. State Privacy Rights

Residents of certain other U.S. states may have similar rights under their own state privacy laws. Rather than apply different rules by state, we offer the same access, correction, and deletion rights described in Section 10 to every Platform user, regardless of where you live.

13. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended for business use by adults and is not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from, anyone under 18 years old. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we will update the "Effective Date" above and, where appropriate, notify active users by email or through the Platform. This will not require existing active users to re-accept the Policy, but continued use of the Platform after a change takes effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.

15. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests regarding your personal information, can be sent to privacy@buyersforce.io.
